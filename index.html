Welcome to Marco Island

A Quiet Creekside Retreat at the Island's Northern Tip

If you are comparing hotels in Marco Island, The Boat House Motel on Marco Island offers something the big resorts cannot: a calm, family-run waterfront setting where the staff greets you by name and the sunset view is part of the room rate. Our property sits on the water at 1180 Edington Pl, just steps from the historic heart of Old Marco.

Whether you arrive by car, by boat, or by rideshare from the airport, you will find tidy rooms, comfortable bedding, and a layout designed for relaxing. Spend mornings with coffee on the deck, afternoons by the pool, and evenings fishing off the dock while pelicans circle overhead.

Many guests tell us they came for one weekend and left planning the next trip. It is easy to see why: a peaceful corner of Southwest Florida, personal service, and a setting that feels like the Florida of old.

FAQ

Frequently Asked Questions

Quick answers before you book.

Where is the motel located?

We are at 1180 Edington Pl, on the northern tip of Marco Island along Collier Creek, close to Old Marco's shops and restaurants.

Can I arrive by boat?

Yes. We have docks and a few boat slips for guests. Call ahead at +1 239 642 2400 to confirm space for your vessel.

What is included in the room?

Rooms include Wi-Fi, television, a refrigerator and coffee-making supplies. An ice machine and guest laundry are available on site.

Is the property walkable to dining?

Yes. Several restaurants in Old Marco are within walking distance, and rideshare is easy for trips farther across the island.

How do I book?

Use the Book Now button for live availability, or phone the front desk and our team will help you choose a room.

What are the pet and reservation policies?

Policies can change, so please visit theboathousemotel.com or call us for the latest details before you reserve.
